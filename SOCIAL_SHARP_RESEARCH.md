# SportEdge Public Sharp / Social Research — 2026-10-02

## Purpose

Use public betting communities to learn repeatable decision process, not to copy viral parlays.

SportEdge remains model-first:

Kalshi universe → independent sport model → role/opportunity/variance → fair probability → Kalshi price/EV → correlation/failure map → optional sportsbook + public-sharp confirmation.

No public account, follower count, P&L badge, post, Discord, X thread, Reddit comment, or recent hit may create a leg that the independent SportEdge model does not already support.

## Threads / other social networks

Search did not surface a sufficiently auditable, pre-posted Threads track record that should influence SportEdge. Nothing from Threads was promoted into the model. Future sources must meet the same verification standard rather than being added for platform coverage.

## Verification standard

A bettor is not “tail eligible” just because they are profitable, popular, or recently hot. Existing SportEdge research policy requires, at minimum:

- 100+ decisions,
- at least 90% verifiable pre-posted decisions,
- positive CLV when CLV is available,
- evidence quality >= 0.70,
- complete wins *and* losses rather than winner-only screenshots.

Until a source meets that standard with auditable data, SportEdge uses only the **repeatable methodology lesson**, never the pick itself.

## What changed in the app

The public-sharp layer now computes a **methodology-fit score** for already model-qualified legs. It rewards:
- high involvement,
- low/survivable variance,
- clear role/availability,
- context-rich model reasons (role, lineup, matchup, surface, serve/return, fatigue, workload, etc.),
- strong model confidence,
- meaningful but non-extreme model-vs-Kalshi price gaps.

It penalizes:
- high-variance legs outside justified longshot mode,
- unresolved role checks,
- extreme model/market gaps without independent confirmation.

This score is a ranking/tie-break input only. It **cannot**:
- create a candidate,
- change SportEdge fair probability,
- turn negative EV positive,
- bypass exact Kalshi contract matching,
- bypass role/injury gates,
- force a leg into a requested multiplier.
